User Guide & Shooting Best Practices

Photo Digitization Guide · Lighting Configuration & Print Standards

1
Batch Import Photos
Drag and drop photos or folders into the workspace. Native support for iPhone HEIC/HEIF format with local decoding.
2
Automated Boundary Detection
Click "Batch Detect" or use shortcuts; the vision engine automatically locates physical photo boundaries.
3
Geometric Refinement
Drag any of the 4 corner anchors to adjust perspective; edge snapping assists in aligning contours.
4
Perspective Deskew & Export
Export to ZIP or local folder. The system rectifies perspective and embeds 300 DPI print metadata.
Guideline 1: Maintain High Contrast
Place light photos on dark backgrounds and dark photos on light backdrops. High contrast significantly improves edge detection accuracy.
Guideline 2: Balanced Diffuse Lighting
Avoid direct overhead harsh light. Use dual 45-degree angle symmetrical lights or soft diffuse lighting to minimize specular reflections.
Guideline 3: Keep Camera Perpendicular
Align your camera parallel to the desktop surface to reduce perspective distortion and preserve effective pixel resolution.
Guideline 4: Leave Separation Spacing
When capturing multiple photos on a single page, leave 1-2 cm spacing between photos to enable clean independent segmentation.
Corner Refinement
Select a crop box and drag anchors to align tightly with physical photo edges.
Rotate (R)
Press R to rotate 90° clockwise; AI automatically detects natural orientation during detection.
Delete Box (Delete)
Select any bounding box and press Delete to remove it.
Batch Box Deletion (Shift + Drag)
Hold Shift and drag an area to select multiple boxes for deletion; press Ctrl+Z to undo anytime.
Navigate Photos (← / →)
Use arrow keys or PageUp/PageDown to switch between photos.
Undo Action (Ctrl + Z)
All geometric modifications and deletions are tracked in history; press Ctrl+Z to revert.
300 DPI Print & Archival Specification
Exported JPEG files automatically embed standard JFIF APP0 density tags (Xdensity=300, Ydensity=300).
Local-Only Processing (Zero-Cloud Leak)
All core algorithms execute entirely on your local machine with zero external cloud dependencies.
